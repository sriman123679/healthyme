# My Thali Planner

A single-page diet, exercise and habit planner for students who want to lose weight slowly and manage a blocked nose and sneezing through food and daily habits.

## Features

- **Today:** a timed day from 7 am to 10 pm with a veg / non-veg switch, meal and habit checkboxes, and a water tracker
- **7-day menu:** Indian home-food meals of about 1,450 kcal a day
- **Exercise:** a beginner, knee-friendly weekly plan with strength routines and an 8-week build-up
- **Sinus care:** foods and habits that may help, and warning signs that need a doctor
- **Food guide:** how to fill your plate and daily nutrition targets
- **Progress:** a weekly weight log with a trend chart

## Install on Android

The app is a Progressive Web App (PWA).

1. Open the GitHub Pages link in **Chrome** on your Android phone.
2. Tap **Install app**, or open the ⋮ menu and choose **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.
3. It opens full-screen from its own icon and keeps working offline.

On a computer, Chrome and Edge show an install icon in the address bar.

## Updating

After changing `index.html`, increase `CACHE_VERSION` in `sw.js` (for example `thali-v3`) so installed copies pick up the new version.

## Privacy

There is no server, account or tracking. Ticks, water counts and weight entries are saved only in the visitor's own browser (`localStorage`).

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the GitHub Pages site.

## Disclaimer

This is general guidance, not medical advice. Teenagers and anyone with a health condition should check with a doctor or registered dietitian before changing their diet or exercise.
