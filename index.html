    Beginner · knee-friendly · no gym

    Workout

    Strength routines

    Rest 45–60 seconds between sets. Breathe out as you push or stand up, and never hold your breath.

    Build up week by week

    Weeks 1–2Get started

    Walk 20–25 min at an easy pace. 1 set of each exercise. 5,000–6,000 steps a day.

    Weeks 3–4Get brisk

    Walk 30–35 min, fast enough that you can talk but not sing. 2 sets. 6,000–7,000 steps.

    Weeks 5–8Add intervals

    Walk 40–45 min, alternating 1 min fast and 2 min normal. 2–3 sets. 7,000–8,000 steps.

    Week 9+Level up

    Do 3 sets of the harder versions. Try cycling or swimming. 8,000–10,000 steps.

    During college hours

    • After every 45–60 minutes of sitting, stand, stretch and walk for 2 minutes
    • Take the stairs, not the lift
    • Walk while you talk on the phone
    • Walk for 10 minutes after lunch

    Breathing for your sinus

    • Humming breath (bhramari): breathe in through the nose and hum softly as you breathe out, 5–7 rounds
    • Alternate-nostril breathing (nadi shodhana): breathe slowly through one nostril at a time for 3 minutes. Skip it if one side is fully blocked.
    • Do these after steam, and never force your breath

    Stay safe

    • Warm up for 5 minutes and cool down with 5 minutes of stretching
    • Stop and tell an adult or doctor if you feel chest pain, dizziness, severe breathlessness or wheezing
    • With a fever or body aches, rest. With only a mild blocked nose, a light walk is fine.
    • On cold or dusty days, exercise indoors or cover your nose and mouth
    • Exercise does not mean you should eat extra. Keep to your meal plan.
    Sinus & food

    Health tips

    These habits can ease a blocked nose and sneezing. They don't cure sinusitis or allergies, so see an ENT doctor if symptoms keep coming back.

    Helps

    • Warm water, clear soups and milagu rasam through the day
    • 1–2 cups of inji-thulasi tea (ginger and holy basil) without sugar
    • Turmeric, garlic, black pepper and ginger in your cooking
    • Vitamin C fruits every day: nellikai (amla), koyya (guava), orange, lemon
    • Pepper, inji (ginger), poondu (garlic) and manjal (turmeric) in your cooking
    • 5–10 minutes of steam, morning and night. Keep your face a safe distance from the water.
    • Sleep with your head slightly raised, for 8–9 hours

    Avoid or limit

    • Ice-cold water, ice cream, cold drinks, chilled juices
    • Fried snacks, bakery items, sugary sweets
    • Thayir (curd) or mor (buttermilk) straight from the fridge, or late at night
    • Dust, perfume, incense smoke, and cold wind while riding

    About milk and dairy

    Research does not show that milk causes sinus problems, but some people feel more blocked after cold milk. If you do, have dairy warm and earlier in the day. Don't cut it out completely, because teens need 1,000–1,300 mg of calcium a day. Ragi (kezhvaragu), ellu (sesame), keerai (greens) and paneer also give calcium.

    Frequent sneezing may be an allergy

    Sneezing a lot and a blocked nose in the morning are often signs of allergic rhinitis, triggered by dust mites, pollen or cold air. A doctor can confirm this. Ask your doctor before trying a saline nasal rinse, and don't take allergy tablets without advice.

    See a doctor soon if you have

    • Fever, or pain and swelling around the eyes, cheeks or forehead
    • Symptoms for more than 10 days, or symptoms that improve and then get worse
    • Thick green or yellow mucus along with fever
    • Any change in your vision, or a severe headache

    • Paruppu (dal), sundal: kondakadalai, karamani, pachai payaru
    • Eggs, and chicken without skin or fish, grilled or in curry
    • Small portions of paneer and thayir (curd)
    • Millets: ragi, kambu, thinai, cholam, plus oats and brown rice
    • Keerai (greens) at least 4 days a week
    • Whole fruit, not juice
    • Pottukadalai, boiled verkadalai (peanuts), sundal

    Once a week or less

    • Bajji, bonda, vadai, samosa, murukku, mixture, chips

    • Maggi, pizza, burgers, canteen fried rice

    • Second helpings of white rice, and lemon or tamarind rice with lots of oil
