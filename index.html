    During college hours

    • After every 45–60 minutes of sitting, stand, stretch and walk for 2 minutes

    • Walk while you talk on the phone

    Breathing for your sinus · 5 min

    • Bhramari (humming bee): breathe in through the nose and hum softly as you breathe out, 5–7 rounds. Humming may help air move in the sinuses.
    • Anulom vilom: slow alternate-nostril breathing for 3 minutes. Skip it if one side is fully blocked.
    • Do these after steam, never forcefully

    Stay safe

    • Always warm up for 5 minutes (marching, arm circles) and cool down with 5 minutes of stretching
    • Stop and tell an adult or doctor if you feel chest pain, dizziness, severe breathlessness, or wheezing
    • With a fever or body aches, rest. With only a mild blocked nose, a light walk is fine.
    • On cold or dusty days, walk indoors or cover your nose and mouth with a scarf or mask
    • Wear proper sports shoes and sip water. During your periods, light walking and stretching are fine.
    • Exercise does not mean you should eat extra. Keep to your meal plan.

    Sinus care through food and habits

    These habits can ease a blocked nose and sneezing. They do not cure sinusitis or allergies, so see a doctor (ENT) if symptoms keep coming back.

    Helps

    • Warm water, clear soups and rasam through the day
    • Ginger–tulsi tea without sugar, 1–2 cups
    • Turmeric, garlic, black pepper and ginger in your cooking
    • Vitamin C fruits every day: amla, guava, orange, lemon
    • Steam inhalation for 5–10 minutes, morning and night (keep the water away from your face to avoid burns)
    • Sleeping with your head slightly raised on an extra pillow
    • 8–9 hours of sleep. A tired body gets more colds.

    Avoid or limit

    • Ice-cold water, ice cream, cold drinks, chilled juices
    • Fried snacks, bakery items, sweets with added sugar
    • Eating curd or buttermilk straight from the fridge or late at night. Have them at room temperature at lunch.
    • Dust, perfume, incense smoke and cold wind on two-wheelers. Cover your nose with a scarf or mask.

    About milk and dairy

    Research does not show that milk causes sinus problems. Some people do feel more blocked after cold milk. If you notice that, have dairy warm, earlier in the day, or as curd at lunch. Don't cut it out completely: at your age you need about 1,000–1,300 mg of calcium a day. Ragi, sesame (til), green leafy vegetables and paneer also give calcium.

    Frequent sneezing may be an allergy

    Sneezing a lot and a blocked nose in the morning are often signs of allergic rhinitis, triggered by dust mites, pollen or cold air. A doctor can confirm this and suggest a safe treatment. A saline nasal rinse helps many people, but ask your doctor how to use it correctly. Don't take allergy or decongestant tablets without advice.

    See a doctor soon if you have

    • Fever, or pain and swelling around the eyes, cheeks or forehead
    • Symptoms lasting more than 10 days, or getting better and then worse again
    • Thick green or yellow mucus along with fever
    • Any change in your vision, or a severe headache

    How to build every plate

    The half-quarter-quarter plate

    Eat more

    • Dal, chana, rajma, moong sprouts, besan
    • Eggs, chicken (no skin), fish: grilled, curry or soup
    • Paneer and curd in small portions
    • Jowar, ragi, bajra, oats, brown rice, daliya
    • All vegetables, especially leafy greens
    • Whole fruits (not juice): guava, orange, apple, papaya
    • Roasted chana, makhana, a small handful of peanuts

    Limit to once a week or less

    • Samosa, pakora, chips, puffs, fried chicken
    • Maggi, pizza, burgers, canteen fried rice
    • Soft drinks, packaged juice, milkshakes, sugary chai
    • Biscuits, cakes, mithai, chocolates
    • Large portions of white rice, maida parotta, naan
